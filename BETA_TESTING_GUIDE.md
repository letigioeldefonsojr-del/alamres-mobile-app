# Almares 328 — Beta Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test Almares 328! This is a quick guide to get you up and running and using the app.

## Your role as a beta tester

You're not just a user here — you're helping catch problems before real customers ever see them. That means:

- Use the app the way a real customer actually would — browse, order, check your order status, edit your profile — rather than only poking at one or two screens.
- Try to break things a little. Enter odd input, back out of a screen mid-action, lose your internet connection partway through, log in and out a few times. Real customers will do all of this without meaning to.
- Report anything that feels off, even if you're not sure it's actually a bug — confusing wording, a slow screen, something that looks wrong, not just outright crashes.
- You don't need to test everything in one sitting. A little bit of regular use over the testing period is more useful than one long session.

## What to test

A few specific things worth deliberately trying:

- [ ] Register a new account with email/mobile number + password
- [ ] Register a new account with Google
- [ ] Log out and log back in with each method
- [ ] Check "Keep me logged in," fully close the app (remove it from recent apps), then reopen it — you should land straight on Home, not back at Login
- [ ] Uncheck "Keep me logged in," close and reopen the app — this time you should be asked to log in again
- [ ] Browse categories and products, including ones with flavor/variant options
- [ ] Add items to your cart, change quantities, and remove an item
- [ ] Place a full order from cart through to confirmation — **then let me know you placed one**, so I can update its status from my end and we can both check that the push notification comes through
- [ ] Check the Orders tab and confirm your order shows up with the right details
- [ ] Edit your profile and, if you have one, add/update a saved delivery address
- [ ] Try logging in with the wrong password and confirm you get a clear error message
- [ ] Try registering with an email or mobile number that's already in use
- [ ] When I update your order's status, confirm the push notification arrives and tapping it takes you straight to that order

## Installing the app

Since this is a test build, it isn't on the Play Store — you'll install the APK file directly.

1. Download the `.apk` file onto your phone.
2. When you open it, Android may warn that it's blocking an install from an "unknown source." Tap **Settings** on that warning, allow installs from the app you downloaded it through (Chrome, Files, Drive, etc.), then go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open the app once it's installed.

## Creating an account

From the Login screen, tap **Register here**. You can sign up either way:

- **Email or mobile number + password** — fill in your details and set a password.
- **Sign up with Google** — one tap using your Google account.

If you sign up with Google, the app will ask you to add a mobile number afterward — this is required so the store can reach you about orders.

## Logging in

Each time you open the app, you'll sign in with whichever method you registered with (email/mobile + password, or Google).

There's a **"Keep me logged in"** checkbox on the Login screen — leave it checked and you won't have to log in again every time you reopen the app, even after fully closing it.

## Browsing and ordering

- **Home / Categories tabs** — browse available products.
- Tap a product to see its details, pick a flavor/variant if there is one, and choose a quantity, then **Add to Cart**.
- Open your **Cart** to review items, then **Proceed to Order** to confirm and place it.
- Prices update automatically if a discount starts or ends while you're looking at a product — no need to back out and come back in.

## Tracking your orders

- The **Orders** tab shows your order history and current status.
- Tapping a push notification about an order (if you've allowed notifications) takes you straight to that order's details.
- **Important:** notifications only go out when an order's status gets updated from the admin side — so whenever you place an order, message me so I can update it and we can confirm the notification actually reaches your phone.

## Your profile

From the **Profile** tab you can edit your details, manage saved delivery addresses, and log out.

## About the yellow banner

You may occasionally see a yellow banner at the top of the Home screen with a short update or announcement — that's how I'll reach testers during this beta without needing you to update the app. Tap **Got it** to dismiss it; a new one will only reappear if there's a genuinely new update.
